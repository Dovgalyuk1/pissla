    FIELD NOTES

    three things i have learned doing this every night for eleven months.

    THE WHEEL IS THE MAILBOX

    the wheel is the only part of the car that touches the street, which makes it the only part that belongs to me. everything above it is theirs and i respect the border. that is the whole etiquette, i did not invent it, i just follow it precisely.

    THEY DRIVE UP QUIET

    these things roll in with no engine sound and park behind you while you are working, which is rude and, frankly, a little sinister. i started keeping one ear pointed at the entrance of the lot. it has saved me twice this month.

    SUNGLASSES AT NIGHT

    the cameras on these cars record everything and the owners watch it on their phones in the morning. the glasses are not a style choice, they are equipment. so far nobody has recognised me at the dog park, so the equipment works.
